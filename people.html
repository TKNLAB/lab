Principal Investigator

Dr. Tapan Kumar Nayak

{{ piTitle }} · {{ piDept }}, IIT Delhi

Tapan completed his Ph.D. in Biophysics at IISc Bangalore (2010) and an MSc in Life Sciences at JNU, New Delhi. He spent eight years at SUNY Buffalo as a postdoctoral associate and research assistant professor, studying the energetics of acetylcholine receptor activation with Anthony Auerbach, before joining IIT Delhi in 2018.

His work combines single-channel electrophysiology, mutational analysis, molecular docking and dynamics to quantify how binding energy is converted into receptor conformational change — and to translate that understanding into allosteric therapeutics.
