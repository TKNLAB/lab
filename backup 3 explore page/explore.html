How to read these

Simulations, not recordings.

Every trace on these pages is computed in your browser from a kinetic model — the same mathematics the published papers use — then filtered and given noise the way a real amplifier would. Nothing is drawn by hand, and every number printed on a figure is fitted live to the simulated data.

Each figure names its model and its source. Where a parameter is ours rather than published, the caption says so. These pages are for explanation; our experimental data appear in our papers.
