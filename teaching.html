In the classroom

Quantitative biology, taught from first principles.

Courses emphasise the physical logic behind biological signalling — how thermodynamics, kinetics and structure together explain what a channel or receptor does.

Students learn to read single-channel records, build kinetic models, and connect molecular measurements to physiology. Project rotations bring undergraduates and Masters students directly to the patch-clamp rig.
